# Sherpa Help

Viewers request help with Escape from Tarkov raids. Streamers and volunteer sherpas lead the raids.

## Language

**Twitch identity**:
A viewer identified by a stable Twitch user ID. The viewer can change their Twitch login without becoming a different person.

**Identity mapping**:
The association between a Twitch login and the known Twitch user ID, Discord member, and Escape from Tarkov name. An unverified mapping has no known Twitch user ID.

**Raid review**:
The staff review of a planned raid before its leader calls the requesters and starts the raid.

**Raid details**:
The Discord message that shows a raid's requesters, goals, notes, and available staff actions.
